About

Built from everyday money questions.

I got tired of not knowing where my money went each month, and of return figures that ignore when money was invested. Monthly Logs tracks actual spending by category; Beruang calculates XIRR, an annualized return that accounts for when money goes in and out.

Both tools are designed around Indonesian money workflows: Monthly Logs uses Indonesian ledger labels, and Beruang includes IDX stock quotes.

  • light & fast
  • clear data handling
  • no account for Beruang
