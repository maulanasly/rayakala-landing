About

I build the tools I use.

I got tired of not knowing where my money went each month, and of return figures that ignore timing. So I built a monthly ledger that records actual spending per category, and calculators that report XIRR — the return after accounting for when money went in and out.

Both tools are free and Indonesian-first. Beruang runs without an account: what you type stays in your browser. Monthly Logs signs in with GitHub and keeps your numbers in your own database.

  • light & fast
  • your data stays yours
  • no signup where possible

Projects

Live tools.

Monthly Logs

My monthly budget ledger. Bulanan records what I actually spent per category, Tahunan shows the 12-month picture, and the dashboard totals it up. Entry is keyboard-driven — Enter moves down a row, Ctrl+S saves. Signs in with GitHub; your numbers live in your own database.

Beruang — Kalkulator Investasi

The calculator I open before making investment decisions. XIRR on mutual funds from monthly deposits, stock returns with live IDX prices, deposit maturity dates so nothing rolls over unnoticed. No signup — what you type stays in your browser.
